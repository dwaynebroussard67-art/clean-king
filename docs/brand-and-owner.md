## Public-source checks

Searched the exact display name, “Louisiana Commercial Janitorial,” Andy/Andrew Merchant with commercial cleaning and janitorial terms, and Lafayette, Lake Charles and Baton Rouge variants. Reviewed chamber, industry, social-business and state-filing leads. A similar name or nearby business is not sufficient to merge records.

- **Strongest professional lead:** the Broussard Chamber of Commerce member directory lists **Andrew Merchant** as **Owner** under **Jani King**, in its cleaning-services category in Lafayette. The indexed directory entry identifies *that* business and role; the page did not load directly during this review. It does not identify the Facebook brand as a DBA, establish that Andy and Andrew are the same person, or authorize reuse of a franchise mark. [1](https://business.broussardchamber.net/list/member/jani-king-1380)
- **Secondary cross-check:** the public Jani-King of Lafayette / Lake Charles business listing has business contact details consistent with the Chamber listing. It is for **Jani-King**, not a second independent verification of LCJ ownership. Its recommendations, awards and contact details have not been transferred to this site. [2](https://nextdoor.com/pages/jani-king-of-lafayette-lake-charles-youngsville-la/)
- **Not the owner's biography:** the regional Jani-King service page describes a franchise network. Its years of experience, territory and services should not be attributed to Andy personally or published as LCJ claims. [2](https://www.janiking.com/commercial-cleaning/lafayette/)
- **Legal-name check remains open:** the [Louisiana Secretary of State filing search](https://coraweb.sos.la.gov/CommercialSearch/CommercialSearch.aspx) is an interactive search by entity, filing number or officer. No matching filing was verified here. A Facebook display name does not prove a registered legal name, corporate suffix or relationship between entities.

**Finding:** there is a credible professional lead worth checking with Andy, but no independently verified public biography connecting that record to the exact supplied brand. No other person's history, personal contact details or photo belong on the new site. The site’s service guide describes what a visitor can *ask about*, not an approved specialty catalog.

## Before public launch — ask Andy

1. What is the canonical Facebook business-page URL, preferred professional name/title and owner-approved two- or three-sentence story?
2. What is the legal/trading name? Is the Chamber entry his, and if so, what relationship and wording, if any, may be published? No franchise affiliation is claimed now.
3. Which services, towns/parishes, service hours, business contact channel and hiring opportunities should the website actually list?
4. Can he supply the original vector/transparent logo, confirm publication rights, and provide approved crew/site images or a portrait for use by the company?
